# GameSharp Tennis — Shippable Learning Contract

A content item is launch-eligible only when every applicable field below is
present, internally consistent and manually reviewed.

## Predict journey

- One observable setup, without hidden facts.
- Exactly three connected decisions.
- Four plausible choices per decision; no comic, giveaway or category-mismatched
  distractors.
- One defensible best answer under the stated conditions.
- An explanation that distinguishes the best answer from the alternatives.
- A boundary explaining what evidence would change the call.
- Authored court state and player orientation for every decision.
- One validated replay whose events match all three correct decisions.
- One exact Live Point ID and one exact Playbook pattern ID.
- One relevant Sharpen area.

## Live Point

- Two credible first lines and a meaningful second decision for each line.
- The first recorded decision remains immutable while the player explores the
  alternative.
- Animation, ball contacts, player movement, labels and natural sound are driven
  by one validated event timeline.
- The result explains the read, not merely the outcome.
- Completion opens the exact Playbook pattern promised by Predict.

## Animation

- The visual must demonstrate the decision being asked. A generic stroke or
  generic court path is not evidence.
- Orientation, service boxes, court geometry, singles/doubles roles, ball depth,
  bounce and player movement must match the copy.
- The same authored state drives card view and Focus View.
- Focus View must preserve the lesson title, fill the useful viewport, respect
  safe areas and never enlarge an unapproved renderer.
- If the approved renderer or authored state is missing, withhold the item. Do
  not fall back to a different animation family.

## Fix the Culprit

- The prompt diagnoses a visible causal fault—not a tactic relabelled as a stroke
  fault and not an answer that repeats the setup.
- The animation visibly contrasts the suspected cause and correction with the
  same camera, timing and body orientation.
- Every answer belongs to that diagnosis. Tactical alternatives, technique
  faults and outcome descriptions may not be mixed as interchangeable options.
- Until these conditions are met and manually signed off, the item is not
  eligible for the Daily Challenge.

## Sharpen concern route

- One short observable concern and one invitation to inspect a useful distinction.
- An exact approved built lesson ID; no nearest-match, unavailable or research-only
  destination. The invitation must fit that lesson's stroke side and stated limits.
- Two or three choices per area, not coverage of every possible symptom or lesson.
- Static preview derived from the approved first scene, never independent geometry
  or a diagnostic claim. A neutral tile is safer than borrowing a singleton renderer.
- Teaching, memory and on-court cue come from the lesson's canonical source.
- A completed lesson can be recorded as seen. Physical practice requires explicit
  self-report; saving and replays cannot create that report.
- Failed assets or unmatched concerns withhold recommendations without substitutes.
- Exact completion, early-exit, saved-focus and Daily-return behavior is browser-tested.

## Source-informed court experiments

The four beta court experiments are bound by exact ID to the approved Contact,
Return Position, Miss Two Points and Approach Volley spines. The shared registry
withholds an experiment if its exact lesson, source, visual evidence or spine
binding is unavailable. It must not change the lesson's memory, observation cue, three decisions or
visual evidence. Its additional practical cue, setup, limits and interpretation
live in one shared definition, with primary sources and an explicit record of the
original adaptation. Source authorship is not independent approval of this app's
exact protocol. `coachApproved` remains false until a real review is recorded.

Do not infer a player's technical cause from a miss, a lesson answer, or one
before-and-after comparison. Count all net/out misses in each ten-ball group;
fewer net misses must not hide more long/wide misses. Never instruct a deliberately
faulty baseline, forced reach/distance, grip or swing changes. Uncertain contact
observations get observation-only guidance. Changed or uncertain conditions
produce an inconclusive reading. Saving/replaying cannot fabricate a court trial.

Return and Volley use three paired comparisons in total, not the forehand's
twenty-ball protocol. Subjective extra return time does not prove better contact
or a better overall return; extra misses and shorter returns remain visible.
A higher volley alone is not a better construction: include approach misses,
absent volleys and the receiver's actual contact. Mental checks use a natural
post-miss opportunity and a ball-responsive intention; never manufacture errors,
count point wins as routine success or mark an unattempted reset as practice.
Known costs and explicit non-attempts remain acknowledged when other observations
are uncertain. All protocols are original, source-informed adaptations, not
independently coach-validated interventions. User design acceptance of forehand
does not confer coaching approval on it or the three additions.

Production eligibility requires explicit review of the intervention and the full
user path; machine-readable structural validity is not coaching sign-off.
On 14 September the user explicitly requested “deploy all” following the four-path
local review and notice of pending independent coach review. This authorises
those four source-informed beta checks to be public while `coachApproved` remains
false. It does not authorise further checks, relax exact evidence audits or imply
that an individual cause or lasting improvement has been established.

## Manual approval record

Every launch-eligible item needs an entry in `LAUNCH_MANIFEST.json` with:

- stable ID and version;
- approved renderer;
- Live Point and Playbook IDs where applicable;
- mobile and desktop review status;
- reviewer note and approval date.
