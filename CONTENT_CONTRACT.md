# GameSharp Tennis — 3D learning content contract

This migration imports the live, previously approved authored lessons unchanged.
It grants no new coaching or physical-technique certification.

- A question's situation, available choices, correct answer, scene and explanation
  must agree. Do not substitute a merely related scene or clip.
- Correct choices must not silently end as lost points. Check all scene branches,
  movement continuity and desktop/mobile framing with `gs/gate.mjs`.
- Keep authored option ordering, clip identity, catalogue membership and exact
  Predict navigation; do not generate replacements during migration.
- Preserve Pro Lens, Coach's Corner, source links, court cue and save behavior.
  Keep observed learning, saved cues and actual on-court practice distinct.
- The existing motion data is adapted from Tennis-MoCap. Retain attribution,
  license and adaptation notices; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Sound is synthesized by `app_src/sfx.js`; no legacy licensed-audio directory
  is imported. Keep reduced-motion and sound controls coherent.
- New eligibility, technical advice or claims need scoped content and visual
  review; structural passing checks alone cannot approve them.
