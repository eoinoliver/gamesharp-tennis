# GameSharp Tennis — 3D product contract

The live 3D lesson app is the product. Migration preserves its exact approved
behavior; it does not revive the historical September 2D application.

- Preserve the cinematic Home, first-visit auto-start/intro, return-to-Home
  behavior, daily completion/countdown and local progress.
- Keep all 21 Daily and two Predict lessons in their approved catalogue order.
- Preserve lesson situation, visual evidence, choices, explanations, replay,
  payoff, saved cue, Home navigation and Sharpen as one coherent journey.
- Keep the seven Sharpen areas and existing stroke/grip cards and images.
- Preserve mobile readability and desktop coherence. Verify representative
  small/large mobile views and desktop; automated checks do not prove physical
  device compatibility or the tennis meaning of every frame.
- Keep production analytics restricted to the two approved HTTPS domains;
  preview/local QA must not send production events.
- A migration is complete only after independent review, Eoin's exact-version
  “ship it”, approved merge, staged production verification and live verification.

For feature work after migration, derive scope from current user decisions;
existing content is not permission for additional diagnoses or efficacy claims.
