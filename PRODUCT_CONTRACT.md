# GameSharp Tennis — Product Contract

## Promise

Every interaction should help a player see the game more clearly, feel smarter
about the next decision, and want to return tomorrow. Trust is the accumulated
feeling that the question, answer, explanation, animation and next step all tell
the same tennis truth.

Trust, useful discovery and delight must survive together. Do not turn uncertain
content into obvious safe-answer quizzes, repetitive advice or sterile copy.
Every full lesson must earn its length through a recognisable player problem or
meaningful gain, observable evidence and a transferable insight. Named Pro
Insights supply human, memorable reinforcement and must fit that exact lesson.

## Agreed Daily direction — September 2026

Gold Daily is intended to become the centrepiece of Daily Challenge: one tennis
insight through three connected decisions, with meaningful contrast, genuine
transfer, a concise named-player payoff and one on-court cue. The common shell
must permit the evidence appropriate to each lesson, including mental behaviour,
return position, movement timing and decision quality.

The approved coaching-card hierarchy (13 September) is shared by every lesson:
readable situation, then visual evidence, then the question and four choices.
Use the lesson title and simple numbered progress, not tiny authoring phase labels.
Choices remain available but visually quieter until the evidence settles. Preserve
readability and touch targets; allow scrolling on short screens or enlarged text.

The accepted expansion direction is to prove the four varied additions, finish
the authored curriculum in audited batches and expand toward an initial 40–60
strong lessons. This is conditional on quality, not a generation quota. The
earlier 12–15 lesson replacement threshold was a recommendation, not an agreed
launch gate. Scheduling, continuity and exact cross-module connections require
implementation before the main Daily can be described as integrated.

The user authorised free lesson selection on 14 September 2026. A secondary

## Sharpen — recognise, learn, take to court

Preserve the approved illustrated player and seven area hotspots. Each area offers
only two or three short, recognisable situations, with quiet static previews.
Choosing one enters its exact approved Daily lesson directly. No second catalogue,
diagnostic questionnaire, long prescription or generic question-bank fallback.

Sharpen borrows the lesson's actual renderer, three decisions, contrast, boundary
and named-player payoff. Its court cue is the canonical lesson memory and on-court
action, not another paraphrased teaching system. Daily can retain that exact cue in
Sharpen without mapping it to a merely related lesson.

An unavailable or unmatched concern is acknowledged honestly. A symptom cannot
prove its cause. Fix the Culprit and legacy question-bank content remain outside
this flow until independently approved under the content contract.

Opening, completing, saving and trying on court are distinct events. Sharpen
practice never changes Daily credit/checkpoints. Save one focus locally; show
on-court feedback only after the player explicitly reports trying it. This is
self-report, not physical mastery or evidence that a technical fault was fixed.
Back and replay must preserve the actual entry context. Old history remains intact
without being relabelled as new practice or improvement.

## Court-check beta — 14 September 2026

The user authorised one complete forehand-error path inside Sharpen, before any
broad Fix the Culprit overhaul. This is a local review exception to the direct
lesson-to-cue flow, not approval of the legacy diagnosis bank or of a live
technical prescription. The user subsequently accepted the forehand design and
authorised three further local journeys: rushed fast-serve returns, the decision
after a miss, and approach-to-first-volley construction. All four use one shared
recognition → plan → explicit court report → neutral reading architecture.
After the local review, the user explicitly requested “deploy all”. This
authorises all four as a public beta through ordinary Sharpen navigation,
without a query flag. A fixed four-ID release list and the exact content audits
control eligibility; adding a new definition does not automatically release it.
The earlier loopback/query restriction is superseded for these four only.

The existing Contact lesson remains unchanged. Its local concern entry uses plain
pain language, then the actual three-decision lesson. A single post-lesson
recognition choice separates repeated crowding from uncertainty or no match.
Only a matching observation offers the source-informed spacing comparison.
Uncertainty and a mismatch do not prescribe a stroke change.

Saving the experiment is not doing it. Two explicit miss counts, a contact
observation and comparable-condition report are distinct from quiz performance.
The first submitted comparison stays immutable; instructions remain readable.
No result proves causation, mastery or durable improvement. Four court experiments
reuse four existing lessons; they are not new lessons or a tracking dashboard.
Return and Volley compare three pairs in total and retain the costs of a change.
Mental records preparation and intention, not point wins; no opportunity or no
attempt cannot record that reset as practiced. Each check retains its own first
report; the original forehand storage is unchanged, with no migration.
Independent coach review remains pending. Production authorisation comes from
the user's explicit release decision, not from structural test results; beta
disclosures and uncertain/nonmatching exits remain visible. This is not approval
of the legacy diagnosis bank or permission to claim validated effectiveness.

## Launch spine

The existing Predict learning journey is:

1. **Predict the Point** — notice the evidence and make three connected calls.
2. **Live Point** — play the consequence and compare the other credible line.
3. **The Playbook** — retain the named pattern and revisit it later.
4. **Sharpen** — work on the relevant game area without losing navigation context.

The Home screen remains calm: Today’s Challenge is primary and Predict the Point
is secondary. Live Point and Playbook are the depth of Predict, not competing
home doors.

## Launch priorities

1. Mobile clarity and reliable navigation.
2. Tennis and learning synchrony.
3. A complete Predict → Live Point → Playbook journey.
4. Natural, timeline-synchronised sensory feedback.
5. Emotional reinforcement through concise, defensible player or coach insight.
6. Desktop coherence; desktop feature richness may wait.

## Scope discipline

- A smaller exceptional product is preferable to a broad inconsistent one.
- Unreviewed content remains unavailable, even when technically renderable.
- Fix the Culprit is quarantined from the Daily Challenge until each diagnosis
  has an approved causal question and a visual capable of showing that cause.
- Old diagram and motion engines remain unavailable to launch content until
  explicitly approved or removed.
- Expansion happens in complete batches of end-to-end journeys, not isolated
  questions, animations or destinations.

## Truthful progress

The product may record that a situation was seen, read correctly, countered in
Live Point or recalled later. It must not infer physical stroke mastery from quiz
activity.
