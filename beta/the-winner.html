How it’s made

Every swing is a real player’s motion, captured in a lab. Every ball follows real flight and bounce physics, so what happens after your choice isn’t scripted — it’s what that shot would actually do. Tap the court to pause.

Still rough: players glide between shots instead of running, and the opponent reuses the same captured swings.

Motion data: Tennis-MoCap, J.D. Pulgarín-Giraldo et al., licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. Pulgarín-Giraldo J.D. et al., A Similarity Indicator for Differentiating Kinematic Performance Between Qualified Tennis Players, LNCS 10125, pp. 309–317, 2017.
