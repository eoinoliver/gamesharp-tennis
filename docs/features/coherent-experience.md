# Coherent tennis experience — 2 November 2026

## Problem and behavior

Home used a separate decorative court/ball renderer and the ball reset in view. Sharpen retained a background court under its controls, automatically cycled strokes, and presented three small reference images. Lessons combined action with stacked slow motion and timed word reveals, then forced another replay before completion. This made the app feel inconsistent and longer than the decisions warranted.

1. Home now uses the same figure, clips, court colours, ball and projection as Sharpen. The complete serve crosses the net, lands and exits the view before a brief scene reset. The court sits above/beside the recommended challenge without obscuring the flight; logo, sound and player level remain accessible.
2. Sharpen starts with its seven concern areas on plain deep green. One chosen stroke stays selected. Replay, Slow view and Pause are explicit; reduced motion starts still. One enlarged preparation/contact/finish reference is visible at a time. The 21 reference images are regenerated at 840 × 798.
3. Authored action runs at normal speed. Whole question text and choices appear after action; extra word-by-word gating and automatic ending slowdowns are removed. Completion does not replay the point automatically.
4. Existing licensed recorded contacts and bounces follow actual visible court events. Explicit replays retain court sound without repeated narration. Mute, first gesture activation and voice preferences remain. No synthesized sound, music or ornamental particle effects are added.
5. Completion foregrounds one court cue, the Live Point turning point/comparison and a next challenge. Optional replay/context/practice and progress/feedback disclosures retain drills, save, restart, share and feedback.

## Audit repairs included

- Saturday Point 1 safely handles a terminal mark with no text: both previously failing options can reach feedback and continue.
- Fixed model patterns disclose the reset after a non-model answer; the original response remains saved.
- Short Ball coaching consistently conditions the approach on balance, contact height and position earned.

## Scope and limits

All 47 existing journeys and seven reference strokes remain available. No new journey is made eligible; no existing journey is withheld. First-response scoring, next-day variation eligibility, local progress and production analytics boundaries are preserved. Prior serve/volley motion corrections remain; this is not independent coach validation or a physical-device audio certification.

## Verification

The release suite includes a new responsive experience check (Home touch targets and overflow, Sharpen phase/motion controls, every Saturday option and four completion journeys). Existing tests now open the optional disclosures through their visible controls and test player-requested replay instead of the removed forced replay. Sound assertions require authored events and no narration overlap. Historical source/release fingerprints are preserved through a new explicit overlay.
