# Catalogue direction

Extends the reviewed two-lesson pilot to the 63 existing journeys. No new lesson
eligibility, motion assets, authored option order, scores, audio or outcomes.
The social showcase remains independent and untouched.

Every choice has a stable identifying colour, matching the on-court letter when
that question has targets. Standard A/B/C/D are orange/purple/teal/gold; the
reviewed Winner wn2 palette remains teal/orange/gold/purple. These are identities,
not grades. Recommendations appear only after commitment as **GS ✓**, with an
accessible recommendation label. Also-valid choices retain their original
feedback and scoring; the tick identifies the authored preferred choice.

Feedback shows the viewed choice and the recommendation, once when they coincide.
Explore other shots (or answers for observations) reveals the remaining options.
The saved first answer stays labelled. Exploring preserves answers, branch nodes,
completion and score, including connected Live Points. Two-answer feedback hides
Explore when both options are already visible. Reduced motion disables the pulse.

## Direction by format

- Daily and connected points: first-visit setup sweep; play the full selected
  consequence; rewind; hold the deciding detail; play the existing alternative.
  Full-court views include all actors and the ball, including both doubles pairs.
- Contact Clue and Late Is the Culprit: body-to-ball relationship at authored
  contact. High Ball, Short Ball and the low/high volley Live Points: contact
  height. The original pilot's measured height and line-margin shots remain.
- Recovery and defensive coverage: both players remain visible, with ground
  markers at their actual positions. Doubles highlights all four positions.
  Other tactical decisions keep the opponent and available court visible.
  Captions reuse the current decision's authored unlock and take-home text.
- Observation questions: one scene, not fictional outcomes per answer. Tiles
  review the authored explanations; Replay the evidence replays that scene.
  Readiness retains its fixed paired camera and previous/current arrival controls.
- Predict: all reads still precede the worked example and recommendations. Choice
  colours follow the target and drawn route. The final review has compact tiles,
  GS ticks and optional individual-shot or sequential comparison replay.

Every directed sequence has Skip; stage and playback pause stop its clock;
background tabs stop progression. Seen openings go directly to the decision.
Repeat answers play only the chosen route. Optional scene replay and comparison
remain. Storage denial is tolerated. Reduced motion uses short routes and static
views. Original completion, voice, coaching, saved cues and navigation remain.

## Verification boundary

Catalogue browser checks exercise real complete journeys on phone and desktop,
first-answer persistence, alternative exploration, connected-route preservation,
Predict review, keyboard controls, compact layout and repeat visits. The original
pilot suite retains measurement, pause, reduced-motion, storage-denial and all
option checks. Existing full release checks remain required. Visual inspection
and exact hosted artifact checks are recorded with the final PR candidate.
Browser checks do not constitute physical-device or learning-effectiveness proof.
