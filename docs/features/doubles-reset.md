# Doubles and Reset

Six complete doubles points extend the established Live Point engine. Three
Reset routines live in Sharpen. The original 57 activities, saved cues, first
attempts and later-day evidence remain intact. No replacement rig or Blender work.

## Doubles: two verified batches

| Family | First situation | Changed situation | Decision that changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serve and cover | They Cross. You Cover. | They Hold. So Do You. | Cover behind a crossing partner; keep your half when they hold. |
| Return and net player | Return Past the Net Player | Now the Line Is Open | Use the available lane, rather than always returning to one target. |
| Lob ownership | Who Takes the Deep Lob? | This Lob Is Yours | Deeper partner retrieves a deep lob; net player takes a reachable overhead. |

Batch 1 was built and exercised through all twelve routes at phone size,
including a separate normal-speed run, before batch 2 was added. Early checks
caught future-choice anticipation and excessive recovery speed; these were fixed
in the authored tracks. Each point has two decisions, four complete routes,
four active players, a controlled first-call comparison, an exact recorded cue,
a sourced coaching insight, and a partner drill. A playable illustrated outcome
is never presented as a guaranteed winner or measured real-world probability.

The shared renderer now draws and sounds contacts for all named actors. Parent
scenes preserve the same preparation before a decision. Fixed broadcast framing
fits all four players and the lob; the existing two-player framing remains intact.
Explicit motion checks cover racket/ball contact, jumps, movement speed and net
clearance. These checks are not independent coaching certification.

Home links to doubles preparation; Explore has a separate Doubles filter;
Sharpen groups the points by three partnership concerns. All six are reachable
immediately. Paired variations also retain the existing later-day practice/read
distinction without forcing players to wait to try them.

Sharing has two separate player actions. The first shares only the point URL,
with no sender answers, score or identity. The second shares an editable proposed
plan, after encouraging the recipient to play independently. Native sharing can
target WhatsApp; copy is the fallback. Cancellation is not reported as success.
There is no account requirement, server pairing, synchronized partner state, or
claim that the partner has agreed. Those are not necessary for this first complete
club-doubles journey.

## Reset in Sharpen

After a miss, before a pressure point, and after partnership tension each have a
specific situation, emotionally recognisable language, an attributed real-world
insight, and a rehearsal. Release → Choose → Commit is GameSharp's authored
framework, not a method attributed to any quoted professional. Three recorded
Frank prompts accompany the eighteen-second guide. Players choose a practical
intention and can pause, advance, restart, mute or save one reminder.

The guide finishing does not save a practice claim. Only “I rehearsed that” saves
a self-report. Reset never writes lesson scores or claims improved performance.
Saved reminders appear in My Game and Reset, stay in this browser, and can be
removed. Unknown/corrupt stored data is preserved; failed writes are not called
successful. Backgrounding pauses the guide and audio; cross-tab muting stops it.

- LTA, [Essential doubles tactics and hand signals](https://www.lta.org.uk/play/tennis-tips-and-techniques/essential-doubles-tactics-and-hand-signals/):
  four active roles, partner-dependent positioning and communication. Point routes

- Peter Farrell, Tennis Ireland, [Doubles — Communication is key](https://www.tennisireland.ie/news/tennis-unlocked-doubles-communication-is-key):
  practise informing the partner of the serve location. The app uses a short
  original paraphrase, not a professional endorsement.
- Dartmouth, [Roger Federer commencement](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2024/06/roger-federer-dartmouth-commencement), 2024:
  Federer's self-reported match/point contrast normalises losing individual points.
- WTA interview with Daria Abramowicz, [How Świątek's sports psychologist honed her mental game](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/1856382/how-swiateks-sports-psychologist-honed-her-mental-game), 2020:
  attention to practical standards rather than demands for a result.
- Gloria B. Solomon, AASP, [Playing Pressure Points](https://appliedsportpsych.org/resources/resources-for-athletes/playing-pressure-points/):
  consistent routines before serving and returning, letting go of the previous
  point, next-point strategy, breath and an attention cue.

No invented professional quotations, likenesses, endorsement, motivational quote
library, universal “percentage mental” claim or clinical/therapy promise.
